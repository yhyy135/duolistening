# Design System: Duolistening

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "One Transcript, Two Readings"**

The Line is what gets studied, and both looks are built to show where the current Line is. **Standard** is the default. It is a music app's lyric page played straight, measured against NetEase Cloud Music and QQ Music. The show's cover sets the mood, only the current Line is drawn at full ink, and its words fill with one blue accent as they are spoken. **Station** is the alternative, and it reads the same Transcript as a railway timetable. Every Line is a stop on one route in route green. Passed stops are filled, the current stop is ringed, and an amber LED strip reports status.

Both looks draw the same DOM (ADR 0016). The look is `data-look="station"` on `<html>`, and when the attribute is absent the page is Standard. The theme is `data-theme="light|dark"`, and when that is absent the system decides. Each device stores both in localStorage. A blocking script in `index.html` applies them before first paint, and Settings → Appearance changes them immediately through `appearance.ts`. Neither is part of the stored Settings, and neither travels in the transfer string. Each look is kept at full fidelity, and neither is a reskin of the other. The product is study, not a game: no streaks, mascots or reward animations.

Density is calm and even. A 60rem column is used on the shelf, a 40rem column in Settings, and Standard's player splits into a side column and the Lines on wide screens. The only motion that carries meaning is the sweep. Every other transition is a courtesy, and reduced motion switches all of them off.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Two looks over one DOM, each in light, dark and auto, chosen per device.
- Every colour is a `light-dark()` token, and rules use aliases rather than a look's palette.
- Standard: a system sans, pills and round transport buttons, real cover art, a blurred cover wash, one blue accent.
- Station: Barlow for Latin, square sign panels, the route in green, amber LED status, a numbering badge per show.
- CJK text is never given a named family. The `lang` on each Line picks the glyph forms.
- Tabular numerals wherever a number changes as you watch.

## Colors

There are two palettes, one per look, each written once with a light and a dark half. A shared set covers cover tiles, part of speech and depth.

Rules use the alias names `--ground`, `--raised`, `--field`, `--thumb`, `--hairline`, `--ink`, `--dim`, `--faint`, `--accent`, `--on-accent`, `--accent-soft`, `--bad` and `--good`. On the bare `:root` they point at `--std-*`, and under `[data-look="station"]` they point at `--sta-*`. The two previews in Settings are the only place that reads the prefixed names directly, because each preview draws its look in that look's own colours whichever look is in force.

### Primary

- **Lyric Blue** (std-accent): Standard's one accent. It is reserved for play, the word fill of the Word being spoken, progress, the focus ring and selection. Chips, the subtitle segment, theme chips and look cards all select in it. Its soft tint (std-accent-soft) marks the Token being spoken, a Line on repeat, the pressed repeat button and text selection.
- **Route Green** (sta-accent): Station's accent. It draws the route line, passed and current stops, the progress band and its train marker, the play button, completed first-run steps, and the selected chip, theme or look card.

### Secondary

- **LED Amber** (sta-led on sta-led-ground, dimmed to sta-led-off): Station's status colour. It is used on the ticker strip under the Lines, the shelf's phase labels, Suggestion badges and the jump-to-current button. The near-black LED ground with sta-on-led also makes the sign-panel headers in Settings.

### Tertiary

- **Failure Red** (std-bad / sta-bad): used only for errors, failed imports, a failed connection check, an armed discard, and Delete.
- **Cover Tiles** (tile-1 to tile-6, text in on-tile): the monogram fallback colour. A show's name fixes which one it gets, so a show keeps its colour on every row.
- **Part of Speech** (pos-noun to pos-interjection): colours Japanese Tokens in both looks. Symbols and "other" inherit the surrounding text colour.

### Neutral

- **Ground / Raised / Field** (std-ground, std-raised, std-field; sta-ground is platform concrete under white sta-raised sign panels): three surface steps, used for the page, cards and docks, then fields and hover.
- **Hairline** (std-hairline / sta-hairline): row dividers, the unplayed track, the route ahead, and Station's inset frames.
- **Ink / Dim / Faint** (std-ink, std-dim, std-faint and the sta equivalents): ink is for the current Line and for titles, dim for secondary text, and faint for Lines not being spoken. Faint is still 4.5:1 against the ground in both themes.
- **Thumb** (std-thumb / sta-thumb): the selected half of a segmented control that does not select in the accent. Station's subtitle modes select in thumb, which is ink.
- **Backdrop / Shadow** (backdrop, shadow): the dimming layer behind a modal, and the one colour every shadow uses.

### Named Rules

**The One Token Rule.** Every colour is a single `light-dark()` token in the palette block. No rule below the palettes names a colour, not even `#fff`.

**The Two Opacity Tokens Rule.** `--pending`, the fade on a Word that has not been spoken (0.55 light, 0.4 dark), and `--wash`, the opacity of the cover wash (0.5 light, 0.6 dark), are not colours. `light-dark()` cannot hold them, so each states both themes the long way, under `prefers-color-scheme` and under `data-theme`.

**The Reserved Accent Rule.** In each look the accent marks playback and selection and nothing decorative. Standard's blue is never put on a heading or a border. Station's green follows the route and is never painted along an edge as a stripe. The same green value also serves as Station's `--good`, and cover art keeps its own colours.

**The One Alarm Rule.** In Station, amber reports state and red means only that something failed.

## Typography

**Standard font:** the system sans (`system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`)
**Station font:** Barlow at 400, 500 and 700, self-hosted as Latin woff2 under SIL OFL 1.1. It is limited to Latin by `unicode-range` and falls back to the same system stack.
**Mono:** `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, monospace`, used only for the settings transfer string.

**Character:** Standard is set the way a music player sets lyrics, heavy and plain. Station is signage: a grotesk descended from Californian highway and transit lettering, with Lines at medium weight and labels at bold or extra-bold.

### Hierarchy

- **Line, wide** (700, 1.625rem, 1.5): Standard's lyric text at 60rem and wider.
- **Line** (700, 1.3125rem, 1.5): Standard's lyric text on phones. Station uses 500, 1.3125rem and 1.6, dropping to 1.125rem under 40rem.
- **Headline** (700, 1.3125rem, -0.01em): section headings on the shelf and in Settings. Station uses 800 with +0.01em.
- **Title** (700, 1rem): top-bar titles, shelf row titles, the Settings legend. Sheet titles are 1.125rem and Standard's side-column episode title is 1.25rem.
- **Body** (400, 15px, 1.5): the base size. Text fields use 16px on touch.
- **Translation** (400, 0.9375rem, 1.5; Station 0.875rem): shown under each Line.
- **Label** (600, 0.8125rem): buttons, segments, field labels, hints and phases. Captions, times and author names are 0.75rem.
- **Status** (Station, 700, 0.75rem, +0.06em, tabular): the LED ticker.
- **Data** (mono, 400, 0.75rem): the settings transfer string, shown whole so a hand-typed copy can be checked.
- **Small** (400, 0.875rem): notices, errors, sheet notes, the search-results line and picker durations. At 600 for chips, Suggestion titles and the rate select. Also Standard's side-column meta, and Station's translations and line counter.
- **Micro** (600, 0.6875rem): key caps and the language-learning badge. In Station, also the badge's language, the labelled transport captions and the LED strip on phones.
- **Brand** (700, 1.0625rem): the app's name in the top bar, and the first-run heading.
- **Monogram** (700, -0.02em): 1.05rem on shelf covers, 1.25rem in the episode picker, 1.5rem on the resume card, 1.75rem on Suggestions and 3.25rem in Standard's side column.
- **Furigana** (500, 0.5em, dim): `rt` over a Token, centred and free to overhang it, so a long reading never spreads the text beneath it. Every Line of a Japanese Transcript carries its readings; only the current Line shows them.

### Named Rules

**The Language Picks the Glyph Rule.** No font stack names a CJK family. Every Line and every translation carries `lang`, and that attribute chooses between Japanese and Chinese forms of the same character.

**The Tabular Clock Rule.** Clocks, counts, rate, Station stamps and the ticker use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, so digits do not shift as they change.

**The Barlow Is Latin Rule.** Barlow is Station's only webfont. Its `unicode-range` keeps it to Latin, and Standard never loads it.

## Layout

The page is a full-height flex column: a top bar, then `main`. There are two scrollers, `main` on the shelf and in Settings, and `.lyrics` in the player. When a dialog is open both are locked with `body:has(dialog[open])`.

- **Shelf:** one column capped at 60rem, with 2.25rem between sections. Suggestions use an auto-fill grid of 8.75rem minimum, which becomes three columns under 36rem.
- **Settings:** one column capped at 40rem, with fieldsets on a grid 1rem apart. The version sits at the right-hand end of the Save row.
- **Standard player, 60rem and wider:** a side column of 19–23rem holds the cover, title, scrubber, transport, modes, rate and shortcut hints. The Lines fill the rest, with a `clamp(2rem, 5vw, 5rem)` gutter between. Below 60rem the cover and title go to the top bar and the controls become a raised bottom dock with 24px top corners.
- **Station player:** the Lines are capped at 54rem. Each Line has three columns: a 3.25rem time stamp, a 2.25rem route column and the text. Under 40rem the time column is dropped and the route moves to the left edge. The dock is full-width and stacks the ticker, previous and next stop names, the band with a tick per Line (up to 150), the times, then the controls. From 44rem the controls sit in one row: modes, transport, rate. Below that they take two rows.
- **Lines** have 45vh of trailing room so the last Line can scroll to the middle. A floating jump-to-current button sits bottom-centre over the Lines.
- **Touch:** under `(hover: none)` every button is at least 44px, and inputs, selects and textareas are 16px. Shortcut hints are shown only with `(hover: hover)` at 60rem and wider. Pinch-zoom is never disabled.
- **Spacing rhythm:** the gaps actually used are 0.375, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5 and 2.25rem.

## Elevation & Depth

**Standard** is soft and lifted in a few places, and every shadow uses `--shadow` with a negative spread, so it pools under the element instead of outlining it. **Station** is flat. Depth there is a 1–1.5px inset hairline frame, which becomes a 3–6px accent inset when selected or current. The only outer shadows it keeps are the sheet's and the scrubber thumb's.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Cover lift** (`0 10px 22px -12px`, `0 12px 24px -16px`, and `0 28px 56px -28px` for the side-column cover): Standard covers only.
- **Accent glow** (`0 12px 24px -14px var(--accent)`): Standard's play and resume buttons. Station removes it.
- **Dock** (`0 -12px 32px -24px`): Standard's phone dock. Station uses `0 -1px 0 var(--hairline)`.
- **Sheet** (`0 30px 70px -24px`): every dialog, in both looks. Station adds an inset hairline.
- **Float** (`0 6px 16px -10px`, `0 10px 26px -12px`): the translating pill and jump-to-current.
- **Frame** (`inset 0 0 0 1px|1.5px var(--hairline)`): Station's panels, chips, fields and inactive stops.

### Named Rules

**The Cover Wash Rule.** Standard's player sits over its cover, blurred 72px, saturated 1.5, scaled 1.35, at `--wash` opacity, and covered by the ground at 74%. The wash never lowers the Lines' contrast. Station draws no wash.

## Shapes

- **Standard** uses soft, rounded shapes. Pills (999px) for buttons, chips, segments, theme chips and the rate select. Circles for icon buttons, transport and play. 10px for fields, 9px for small covers, 12px for shelf and Suggestion covers, 18px for the side-column cover, look cards and fieldsets, 20px for the resume card, 22px for sheets (top corners only on phones), 16px for a Line's hover and repeat background, 11px for the look previews in Settings, 7px for the cover in the player's top bar, and 3px for a Token's sweep highlight.
- **Station** uses square corners. 4px for buttons, chips, fields, cards, sheets and fieldsets; 2px for covers, phase tags and previews; 0 for Lines, the dock and progress bars. Rounding is kept where the metaphor needs it: circular stops, transport and play; the numbering badge's framed sign at 8px (10px on the resume card); labelled transport buttons at 6px; a 5px progress band; a 3px subtitle-segment thumb; 1px rules inside the look preview; and a 4px route.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Shape:** pill in Standard (999px), 4px in Station. Icon, transport and play buttons are circles in both.
- **Default:** `--raised` background, ink text, weight 600, `0.5em 1.05em` padding. Hover moves the background to `--field`, press moves the button down 1px, and a disabled button is at 0.5 opacity.
- **Primary:** accent background with on-accent text. Hover brightens it to 1.06.
- **Ghost:** no background, dim text at weight 500. Hover gives raised and ink.
- **Danger:** bad-coloured text.
- **Focus:** the only focus ring is a 2px accent outline offset 2px, shown on keyboard focus only.
- **Station:** secondary buttons and chips are raised with a 1.5px hairline inset. Hover darkens the inset to faint.

### Chips and segments

- **Chip:** raised background, dim text, 0.875rem at weight 600. Selected is accent with on-accent text in both looks.
- **Subtitle modes** (Both, Original, Hidden): a radio group drawn as its labels. Standard draws a hairline track holding pills and selects in the accent. Station draws a field track with a hairline inset, 3px segments, and selects in thumb (ink). Hidden blurs each Line's text and translation by 0.42em until that Line is revealed.
- **Theme chips and look cards:** select in the accent. A look card shows its selection as a 2px inset ring, 3px in Station.

### Cards / Containers

- **Resume card:** raised, 20px corners, a 4.75rem cover and a round 3rem accent play button that scales to 1.06 on hover. In Station it is a 4px framed panel and shows the numbering badge instead of the cover.
- **Shelf:** rows separated by hairline dividers, showing cover, title and phase. In Station it becomes a departure board: a framed raised panel with no covers, bold titles, LED phase tags, and failures in bare red.
- **Fieldsets (Settings):** raised, 18px corners, 1rem padding. In Station each is a 4px framed panel, and its legend becomes a full-width black sign header.

### Inputs / Fields

- **Style:** field background, 1px transparent border, 10px corners (4px in Station). In Settings forms they use the ground background with a hairline border.
- **Focus:** a 2px accent outline at offset 0. The caret uses the accent too.
- **Key fields** are plain text inputs masked with `-webkit-text-security`, never `type="password"`.

### Navigation

- **Top bar:** at least 3.5rem tall (4rem in Station's player), with a hairline underneath. It is ground-coloured in Standard and raised in Station. Its first control lines up with the content column below. Back is a ghost pill with a chevron and turns red when armed. The brand is 1.0625rem at 700 with the app icon.

### Cover

The show's own artwork, loaded directly from its host with no referrer. When there is none, the same box shows a two-character monogram on a tile colour picked by the show's name: CJK names give their first two characters, other names give initials. The cover is decorative (`aria-hidden`).

### Numbering Badge (Station)

A 2.625rem square sign with a 3px route-green frame and 8px corners. The show's monogram is on top (800, 0.8125rem) and the two-letter code of the studied language below it (700, 0.6875rem, dim). It is drawn in the player top bar and on the resume card, where it is 3.25rem with a 4px frame. Standard never draws it.

### The Lines

Each Line is a real `<button>` inside a list item. A Line not being spoken is faint, the current Line is ink, and an ask-AI icon button sits at its right edge. That button appears on hover or when the Line is current on desktop, and is always visible on touch. Lighting a Line never changes its size or its place, in either look. Every Line of a Japanese Transcript carries its furigana, hidden except on the current Line, so a reading holds its room and never makes a Line grow as it lights.

- **Standard:** the current Line lights by colour alone. The Word being spoken turns accent. A pending Word or Token is at `--pending` opacity. The Token being spoken gets an accent-soft background with a 2px soft ring and keeps its part-of-speech colour. A Token with furigana is an inline-block, which Safari needs to draw the reading.
- **Station:** a Line lights by changing colour, never size. The next Line is dim and the current Line and its stamp are ink. The route runs 4px behind the stop column: accent over passed Lines, hairline ahead, and split at the current stop. Stops ahead are hollow 0.875rem rings, passed stops are filled 0.75rem dots, and the current stop is a 1.625rem ring with a 6px accent border. A Line on repeat fills its stop instead of tinting its row.

### Transport and dock

Replay, previous, play, next and loop, in that order. Standard uses 3rem circles and a 3.75rem accent play button with a glow. The scrubber is a 4px track with a 14px thumb, and its played part is a gradient stop at `--played`, which the frame loop writes. Station has a 4rem play button, labelled 3.5rem buttons for replay and repeat, a 10px band with a 26px ringed train marker, 5px ticks below the band, and previous and next stop names above it. Its ticker lists playing state, "Line n of N", repeat and subtitle mode, and on phones it shows only the first three.

### Sheets

Every dialog uses one component. On wide screens it is a centred card, `min(36rem, 100% - 2rem)` wide and at most `min(85dvh, 46rem)` tall. Under 36rem it is a full-width bottom sheet with top corners only, respects the safe-area inset, and slides up over 0.32s. The backdrop colour dims the page behind it. Only the body scrolls, and the title and actions stay fixed.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** add every new colour as one `light-dark()` pair under `--std-` and under `--sta-`, and point an alias at them. Rules should reference the alias.
- **Do** style Station by overriding the same DOM under `:root[data-look="station"]`. Hide parts of one look with `display: none` in the other rather than adding a second structure.
- **Do** keep a 44px minimum on touch targets and 16px on text fields under `(hover: none)`.
- **Do** set `lang` on any text in the studied language, and use `tabular-nums` on any number that changes while playing.
- **Do** make a lit state change colour, a transform, or a box-shadow ring, never a width or padding. In Station, colour only.
- **Do** make every dialog a `.sheet`, and wrap motion so reduced motion disables it.
- **Do** use a show's real artwork, with a monogram tile when there is none.

### Don't:

- **Don't** write a hex value, `rgb()` or a named colour below the palette block.
- **Don't** name a CJK font family in any stack.
- **Don't** use Standard's blue for anything but play, the word fill, progress, focus and selection. Don't use Station's green for anything off the route, play and selection. Don't use red for anything but failure.
- **Don't** round Station's panels, controls or Lines beyond 4px. The only rounded exceptions are stops, circular transport buttons and the numbering badge.
- **Don't** draw a coloured side border to mark a Line or a card as active. Use a filled stop, a badge, an inset ring or the accent fill.
- **Don't** name a class `pending`, `now`, `said`, `passed`, `current` or `next` for anything other than the sweep and Line states.
- **Don't** set `maximum-scale` on the viewport or use `type="password"` on a key field.
- **Don't** add streaks, mascots, reward animations or celebratory motion.
