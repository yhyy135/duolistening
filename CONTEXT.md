# Duolistening

A self-hosted listening-practice tool: import a podcast episode, transcribe it with a user-configured LLM, and study it through a lyrics-style, time-synced bilingual transcript. Everything a reader has — keys, shelf, Transcripts, audio — lives in their own browser (ADR 0008).

## Language

**Resource**:
A podcast episode the user has imported to study. The thing a Transcript belongs to. Carries the language pair it was transcribed in, so later changes to Settings cannot misdescribe it, and its show's title and cover address as the feed gave them, for display. Its audio and its Transcript arrive separately and either can be missing: an episode imported before a Transcription Model was configured has audio and no Lines (ADR 0012), and one restored from a backup has Lines and no audio.
_Avoid_: Track, episode, video, media, source

**Library**:
Everything the user has imported, kept in their browser's IndexedDB until they delete it by hand. Deleting an entry takes its Transcript and audio with it. The landing screen: pick an entry and drop straight back into where you left off.
_Avoid_: History (implies an event log that ages out — a Library entry only leaves when deleted), collection, shelf

**Suggestion**:
A podcast _show_ offered to the reader before anything is imported — its title, its cover art, and the feed URL an import would start from. Never part of the Library: picking one only opens that feed's episode list, and a Resource appears if the reader then chooses an episode from it. Comes from Apple's search endpoint (ADR 0013), refreshed once a day.
_Avoid_: Recommendation (the section is headed that, but one row of it is a Suggestion), result, hit, podcast (ambiguous between a show and an episode)

**Favorite**:
A show the reader starred in its episode list to come back to — its feed URL, and the title, author and cover address the feed gave. Kept in the browser like the Library, but it is a show and not an episode: starring one fetches and imports nothing, and a Resource appears only when an episode is picked from its feed, as with a Suggestion. The home screen shows them under their own chip, newest first, and opens on that chip whenever there are any.
_Avoid_: Subscription (implies new episodes arrive on their own, and nothing is imported without a pick), follow, bookmark

**Transcript**:
The ordered sequence of Lines generated for a Resource, covering its full duration.
_Avoid_: Subtitles, captions (those imply externally-authored text; a Transcript here is generated by the configured Transcription Model)

**Line**:
One timestamped unit of a Transcript — original-language text, its start/end time, and (once translated) the corresponding native-language text. Rendered as one row in the lyrics view; clicking it seeks playback to its start time.
_Avoid_: Segment, sentence, subtitle

**Word**:
One entry in a Line's optional word-level timing data — original-language text plus its own start/end time within the Line, used for karaoke-style highlight during playback. Present only when the Transcription Model returns word-level timestamps.
_Avoid_: Token — a different, timing-free concept (see below)

**Token**:
One morpheme of a Line's text as split by the Japanese morphological analyzer, carrying its part-of-speech and kana reading (for furigana). Purely text-derived, no timestamp, not tied to playback — its boundaries need not match a Line's Words at all. Only present when the Resource's target language is Japanese.
_Avoid_: Word (Word is timing data; Token is linguistic annotation)
